Questions, answered.

Where does the data come from?

Public registers and firms' own websites: Companies House, the SRA register and similar sources. Each list is filtered and checked before it goes out. The sources page names every source and how it is used.

Do I get emails or phone numbers?

No. Each firm comes with the link to its own contact or careers page, so you reach the firm through the channel it chose. We do not sell personal contact details.

Will this get me a job?

We cannot promise that. The lists save you the research and tell you who to approach. The tracker and message templates help you send a good first message and follow up. The outcome depends on your applications.

How often are the lists updated?

Every month. New firms are added and closed ones removed, and each update says what changed.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time, online, in two clicks. You also have a 14-day cancellation right unless you choose immediate access, which the checkout explains before you pay.

Who is behind it?

Two people who built the data tool themselves and use it to research the firms in each list.
